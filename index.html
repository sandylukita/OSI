About PT OSI

Pioneering Industrial Excellence Since 2018

PT Optima Sarana Instrumen (PT OSI) began its business operation as a Consultant, Process/Laboratory Instrumentation, Field Instrument, and Automation solutions providing company in June 2018.

We provide a complete one-stop solution for field Process Analyzers, Liquid Analyzers, Laboratory Instrumentation, Field Measurement Devices, and Industrial Automation. With a deep commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction, we empower enterprise clients across Indonesia to optimize processes, boost efficiency, and achieve operational excellence.
